Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a donor?

Open the Donors section and select Register Donor.

How do I submit a blood request?

Open Blood Requests, select New Request, and enter the required details.

What does Pending mean?

The request is waiting for an administrator to review it.

How can I update blood stock?

Open Blood Inventory, select Add Stock, and enter the blood group and units.
